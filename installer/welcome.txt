Welcome to TradingBot V1 — Trade Command Centre

This setup wizard will install:

  • Trade Command Centre desktop application
  • Ollama (optional) — local AI for trade analysis
  • AI models (optional) — llama3:latest by default
  • MetaTrader 5 (optional) — connect to your broker

After installation:
  1. Open MetaTrader 5 and log in to your broker account
  2. Enable "Algo Trading" on the MT5 toolbar
  3. Launch Trade Command Centre from the Start menu
  4. Click Connect MT5, then Start

Internet connection is required to download Ollama, AI models, and MT5.
